Panjiia

The trusted record rural finance will run on.

Panjiia is the data and intelligence layer of DNC. It turns a single maize purchase into a verified record, a farmer registry and, over time, one of the most valuable agricultural datasets in Zambia.

What it is

Not an app. The infrastructure underneath the work.

Panjiia is how a maize purchase in a village becomes a verified record, a farmer registry, a credit profile and, in time, a doorway to finance and services. It is built data-first, so that everything above it stands on solid ground.

The ecosystem

Every purchase connects the whole chain.

Farmers, field agents, warehouses, buyers and finance all meet on one platform.

Built in disciplined phases

Each phase stands on its own before the next.

Foundation

Already built. Field operations, a unified farmer registry, GPS-tagged purchases and live operational visibility. This runs the business today.

Platform

Next. Credit profiles from transaction history, payments, and input finance repaid in grain, delivered through licensed partners.

Intelligence

The horizon. Better farming decisions, protection against shocks, and a widening range of services within reach of every farmer.

The moat

Every transaction makes the system smarter.

No off-the-shelf database of Zambia's smallholder farmers exists to buy. DNC builds its own, verified transaction by transaction. The more we trade, the more we learn; the more we learn, the more value we can return to the farmer. That compounding, trusted dataset is the hardest thing for anyone else to replicate, and it is what turns a grain trade into an institution.
