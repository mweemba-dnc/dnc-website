Impact

One fair purchase does not stop at the farm gate.

It pays school fees, it hires a neighbour, it keeps money inside a district instead of leaking out of it. We build so that the compounding happens, and we record so that it can be checked.

From one purchase

How a single fair purchase compounds.

This is the argument for doing the unglamorous part properly. Nothing further along the chain happens unless the first step is real.

Who this reaches

The household, the district, and the people the system has never counted.

The household

A fair price paid on the day, with no broker and no queue. Behind each farmer there is a household, and the income arrives for all of them.

Women farmers

A verified record in a farmer’s own name, not their husband’s. Where land and assets sit with someone else, the record is the first thing that is genuinely theirs.

The district

Field agents, warehouse and transport work, and procurement that stays local. The bricks in the store were made by people who live beside it.

The global goals

Aligned, and we would rather be specific about how.

A row of coloured tiles is the easiest thing in the world to put on a website. We would rather describe the mechanism, then name the targets the mechanism actually acts on.

Decent work and economic growth

Salaried seasonal roles above the statutory floor, covered by the schemes they carry. Target 8.5.

Gender equality

Equal access to economic resources, because the record gives a woman farmer a credit history in her own name. Target 5.a.

No poverty

Nationally appropriate social protection systems, including floors, which is the target the social protection programme is built on. Target 1.3.

Good health and well-being

Universal health coverage with financial risk protection, through enrolment at the point of sale. Target 3.8.

Zero hunger

Doubling the productivity and incomes of small-scale food producers, through the finance and infrastructure rungs. Target 2.3.

Quality education, indirectly

A consequence of household income and protection, not a result we claim or measure. Target 4.1, marked indirect on purpose.

Where we work

One district, done properly. Then it travels.

We operate in Petauke District, in Zambia’s Eastern Province. One district, done properly, is the whole strategy, because a model that has not been proved somewhere small has no business being carried anywhere large. What is being built here is not specific to Zambia. The absence of a verified record is the same problem across sub-Saharan Africa, and the rail is designed to travel.

The shaded continent is the scale of the problem, not a claim about where DNC operates. We work in one district.

Where we are headed

Stated as intent, so there is no confusion with what is true today.

Intent

A district that can finance itself

Enough farmers with a verified history that an institution can build a channel to them, rather than one more pilot that ends with an article.

Intent

A prosperity centre

Somewhere in the district where people learn, heal and gather. Collective infrastructure comes before commercial cooperation, which is the lesson we took from how this was done elsewhere.

Ambition

A stake held for the people who built it

Genuine ownership, through a trust or through patronage, is a destination we treat as credible and desirable. It is not the opening claim, and we will not show a farmer a number before there is money standing behind it.

We would rather report a smaller, true number than a large, soft one. Nothing on this page is a projection, and we never claim an income or yield uplift we have not measured.

The infrastructure remains long after the funding ends.
