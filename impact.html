Impact

Impact you can prove, not just claim.

Because every transaction is recorded and verified, DNC can show exactly who was reached, where, and how. Evidence is not an afterthought here. It is built into the model, and it is the foundation of the record we publish over time.

How every purchase works

The difference between claiming impact and proving it.

Most organisations describe their impact. DNC records it. Every purchase is GPS-tagged and verified, which means the impact can be measured, audited and, in time, published as evidence for governments, funders and partners.
