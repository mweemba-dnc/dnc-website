DNC is a young organisation with a long horizon. We are always interested in people who want to help build rural infrastructure that will outlive us.

Working at DNC

Purpose, discipline, and the long view.

We do the unglamorous work first: buying maize, paying farmers fairly and fast, recording every transaction. We value integrity over easy money, evidence over guesswork, and the long view over the quick win. If that is how you work, we would like to know you.

We do not always have open roles, but we always read thoughtful introductions, especially from people with experience in agribusiness, field operations, data and engineering, development finance, and impact measurement.
